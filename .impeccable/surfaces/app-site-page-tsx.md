# Homepage surface brief

Scope: the homepage (`/`), Persuade mode. One design, The Chambers Seal, built code-first (no image generation). The Registry Ledger and The Kimana Counsel variants and the `?design=` switcher were tried and dropped by the owner.

Audience and job: families and individuals in Kajiado South with land, succession or family matters, and conservancies, NGOs, SMEs and diaspora investors vetting the firm remotely. Action: call, WhatsApp, or request a consultation. Proof: the real headline, the three courts, the five practice areas, and the firm's own mark. No stats, testimonials or client logos until confirmed. Constraints: no boasting or outcome promises, light pages for slow phones, longer Kiswahili strings must fit, the Kiswahili toggle stays cosmetic.

Unresolved: client sign-off on the audience-entry wording and the prefilled WhatsApp text; a vector logo and real photography are still needed from the client.

## Direction contract: seal (The Chambers Seal)

THESIS: the homepage as a sealed deed. Navy authority, ivory paper, and gold used only as a mark, refusing the generic centred-hero-then-card-grid arrangement by putting two instruction sheets at the fold.
OWN-WORLD: committed navy (#0b1424, #10192e) carrying about half the surface, ivory reading bands, gold-seal line work, Cormorant Garamond and EB Garamond, square hairline cells, and the serrated gold-seal roundel taken from the firm's own seal as the one recurring device.
STORY: the visitor understands this is a formal, discreet Kajiado firm, sees two clear doors (individuals, institutions), and calls or messages.
FIRST VIEWPORT: full-bleed navy with the seal photograph as texture, headline bottom-left at display scale, Call and WhatsApp buttons directly beneath, header transparent over it; two ivory instruction-sheet panels begin at the fold.
FORM: user-pinned "The Chambers Seal" (position n/a, pinned beats the roll), seed key 145ac723. Signature moment: the serrated seal presses onto the closing band once when it enters view.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
